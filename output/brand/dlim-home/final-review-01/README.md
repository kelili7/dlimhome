## Visual QA and usage notes

- Compared generated outputs against selected A: recognizable DL silhouette, open internal channel, long rising L foot, D arch, and sans-serif DLIM Home naming are retained at visual review level. Generative raster outputs are not pixel-identical to source.
- Source image remains authoritative. Do not extract a tiny logo from the board or replace source with a mockup imprint.
- Exact spelling and casing of DLIM Home reviewed.
- Transparent assets are intended for light backgrounds. Reverse asset is for dark-background demonstration.
- Main provisional colors: ink-brown #342F2B, pure black #000000, pure white #FFFFFF. These labels are intended specifications; generated images may have mild tonal variation and do not constitute calibrated print separations.
- Clear-space proposal: x = one quarter of standalone monogram height; keep at least x around the full logo content bounds. Transparent-file canvas padding is not a measured clear-space template.
- Grid is an analysis aid, not a geometric reconstruction recipe. Curves must follow locked source rather than a newly assigned radius.
- Two-color treatment and avatar variants are review proposals. Complete palette and VI require subsequent approval.
- Small-size panel is an illustration, not a pixel-accurate size test or factory minimum-size certification. Ceramic underside marking needs supplier proofing for actual process and glaze.
- Full VI/applications plan follows final-logo approval; dedicated application images and HTML manual have not been produced at this stage.
