## Locked invariants

- DL monogram with a broad D arch, softly flared top-left terminal, rounded lower-left turn and inward upward taper.
- L stem begins inside the D, with a rounded upper-left shoulder; its sweeping low foot extends right and ends in an upward diagonal taper.
- The D descender/open right terminal and L upright retain their narrow separation and original negative space.
- Preserve silhouette, relative positions, proportions, weight relationships, exact angle/curve character and slight asymmetry.
- Preserve the source sans-serif DLIM Home lettering, exact casing, optical spacing and symbol-to-wordmark scale.

## Allowed refinement

- Background removal, flat ink color, contrast, clean raster export, crop, scale, clear-space framing and approved usage variants.
- Isolating the original monogram as an icon and monochrome/reversed use.
- Diagrams may surround or overlay the existing image but may not reshape it.

## Forbidden

- New geometry, altered counters/negative space, cosmetic redrawing, new typography, smoothing distinctive curves away, stretching, tracing, SVG or code-drawn logo substitutes.
