# DLIM Home — Logo Concept Brief

## Design brief

**Summary:** A ceramic source manufacturer and consumer brand creating cups, plates, bowls, serveware and bakeware for everyday homes and hospitality use.

**Audience:** Online retail customers on Amazon and Taobao; wholesale and custom-product buyers who need to trust the manufacturing source.

**Brand adjectives:** calm, refined, tactile, dependable, contemporary.

**Avoid feeling:** cute-only, rustic-craft cliché, mass-market generic, interior-design studio.

**Promise / big idea:** Thoughtful ceramic forms, made at the source and brought into daily life.

**Competitive landscape:** Ceramic brands often rely on literal cups, steam, pottery hands, script lettering, or generic serif luxury. The open territory is a restrained geometric identifier derived from vessel form, nesting and the kiln-to-home journey.

**Mark type hypothesis:** A combination mark led by a compact letterform or abstract pictorial symbol. The symbol must work alone on backstamps, cartons, marketplace avatars and embossed ceramic bases.

**Must work on:** 16 px store icon, website header, packaging carton, ceramic backstamp, one-colour screen print, emboss/deboss, dark and light backgrounds.

**Constraints:** New identity; exact name is `DLIM Home`; no existing logo or colour equity was supplied. First-round concepts stay monochrome so the decision is about form rather than colour.

**Success criteria:** Recognisable at 16 px; balanced in one colour; relevant to ceramics without drawing a generic cup; premium but approachable; distinctive from the supplied inspiration logos.

**Assumptions:** The logo should support both consumer retail and factory/wholesale credibility. The reference image defines restraint, spacing and blue-grey mood, not a logo to imitate. Final colour and typography will be developed only after a direction is selected.

## Word map

- DLIM Home → D, home, table, place, ritual, gathering
- ceramics → vessel, rim, plate, bowl, kiln, glaze, clay, fired form
- source factory → origin, repetition, nested sets, production, reliability
- premium calm → negative space, measured geometry, quiet weight, tactile detail

## Concept longlist

1. **Vessel D:** a capital D whose counter is a shallow bowl seen in profile.
2. **Source Stack:** three reduced vessel profiles build a stable, repeatable collection.
3. **Kiln & Bowl:** a kiln/home arch contains a bowl in negative space.
4. **Place Setting:** a plate ring interrupted by an L-shaped place marker.
5. **Nested Rim:** concentric rounded rectangles form a D-like platter symbol.
6. **Handle Pair:** mirrored handles create an H and a central table opening.
7. **Table Line:** a custom DLIM wordmark shares one calm horizontal baseline.
8. **Maker's Stamp:** a compact DH monogram designed for ceramic backstamps.
9. **Glaze Horizon:** a split circle captures the meeting of clay body and glaze.
10. **Shared Table:** four arcs gather around a quiet central place.
